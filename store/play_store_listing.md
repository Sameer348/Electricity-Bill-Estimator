## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Want to know what your next electricity bill might be? Electricity Bill Estimator turns the units on your meter into an estimated monthly bill in seconds. It is built for homes and shops in Pakistan.

⚡ FAST BILL ESTIMATE
Enter your units (kWh), choose Residential or Commercial, and tap Calculate. You'll see:
• Your estimated total bill in PKR
• A slab-by-slab breakdown of energy charges
• Additional charges such as surcharges
• Taxes such as GST, electricity duty and PTV fee
• Your average cost per unit

🔌 APPLIANCE CALCULATOR
Find out which appliances push your bill up. Pick a preset (fan, LED bulb, refrigerator, TV, iron, washing machine, air conditioner or water pump) or add your own. Enter wattage, hours per day and days per month to see each appliance's monthly units and estimated cost. You can then send the total to the bill calculator.

📊 EDITABLE TARIFFS
Rates change often, so every slab rate, tax and surcharge can be edited in the app. You can choose progressive slabs or single-slab billing, and reset to the defaults at any time.

🗂️ HISTORY AND SHARING
Save estimates, compare them later, and share a simple text summary with family on WhatsApp or any other app.

🌙 MADE FOR YOU
• English and Urdu (اردو)
• Light, dark and system themes
• Clean, simple design with large, readable numbers

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up or login
• Works fully offline
• No permissions required
• Your data never leaves your phone

IMPORTANT: This app gives ESTIMATES only. It is not affiliated with WAPDA, NEPRA, K-Electric or any DISCO, and it is not an official billing system. Rates are estimates and may change. Your actual bill may differ because of taxes, surcharges, adjustments, meter type, tariff category and government notifications.
